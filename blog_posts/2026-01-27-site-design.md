At the time of writing, this blog is built using Python 3.11.9, with Flask as the web framework, and Frozen-Flask to render it as a static site to minimise hosting costs. My intention is that this blog will become a personal knowledge base, collecting explanations of the challenges I encounter as I develop software and explanations on how to overcome them.
